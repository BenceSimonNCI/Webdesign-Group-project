About the arcade

Our story

Neon Arcade started with three rescued cabinets in a storage unit and a stubborn belief that games are better shared.

Today we run a floor of restored classics alongside modern indie machines, host weekly tournaments and keep the change machine well fed.

House rules

  • No drinks on the machines - bottle holders are on the side of every cab.
  • Winner stays on, but respect the queue card on popular cabs.
  • Under-14s welcome with an adult until 8pm.
  • Be kind to the machines. They are older than you.

FAQ

Walk-ins are always welcome. Bookings are only needed for parties and private hire.

Most of our classics run original boards in restored cabinets; a few run faithful emulation for reliability.

Fight sticks are welcome on tournament nights - ask staff to plug you in.
